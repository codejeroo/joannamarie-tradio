MAGEC 2026 at USTP CDO

The First Bearing: Mindanao Area Geodetic Engineering Congress at USTP CDO

The 1st Mindanao-Area Geodetic Engineering Congress (MAGEC 2026) was held on February 23–25, 2026, at USTP–Cagayan de Oro Campus under the theme “The First Bearing: Mapping Mindanao’s Next Generation of Surveyors.” The three-day congress brought together Geodetic Engineering students and institutions across Mindanao for knowledge exchange, collaboration, professional engagement, and unity in advancing the profession.

KAALAM 1

360 Cameras in Geomatics: A Game Changer for Indoor Mapping

Tech Spotlight: KAALAM 1 featured “360 Cameras in Geomatics: A Game Changer for Indoor Mapping,” highlighting the use of 360° imaging technology in modern surveying and mapping. The session emphasized its potential for efficient indoor data collection, spatial documentation, visualization, and improved geomatics workflows, showcasing how emerging technologies continue to expand the capabilities of the profession.

KAALAM 2

Exploring the Expanding Horizons of Geodetic Engineering Beyond Field Survey

Future Horizons: KAALAM 2 featured the technical session “Exploring the Expanding Horizons of Geodetic Engineering Beyond Field Survey,” highlighting the diverse applications and emerging opportunities in the profession. The session emphasized how geodetic engineering continues to evolve through advanced technologies, geospatial applications, and innovative practices beyond traditional field surveying.
